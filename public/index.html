Is Our Care Program Right For You?

Check if you or a family member are experiencing any of these chronic pain indicators:

☑ Knee Pain & Stiffness: Difficulty climbing stairs, squatting, or standing up after prolonged sitting.
☑ Joint Friction & Crepitus: Grinding sounds, clicking, or localized morning joint heat and swelling.
☑ Sciatica or Lumbar Disc Bulge: Lower back ache shooting down the hip, thigh, calf, or foot.
☑ Cervical / Desk Strain: Severe neck stiffness, shoulder spasms, or radiating arm numbness from screen work.

☑ Painkiller Dependency: Relief wears off as soon as pills stop; trapped in a recurring cycle of inflammation.
