The Living Tradition of Kerala Ayurveda

Centuries of Pure Kerala Healing,

Kerala is revered globally as the cradle of authentic Ayurveda. For over a millennium, the 18 hereditary physician families (Ashtavaidyas) preserved the classical Ashtanga Hridaya and developed non-surgical, curative therapies found nowhere else in the world.

"प्रयोगः शमयेद् व्याधिमेकं योऽन्यमुदीरयेत् । नाऽसौ विशुद्धः शुद्धस्तु शमयेद्यो न कोपयेत् ॥"
"The therapy that pacifies one symptom while producing another illness is NOT true medicine. Pure Ayurveda cures the disease at its metabolic root without creating secondary disorders."
— Ashtanga Hridayam • Classical Canon of Kerala Ayurveda
