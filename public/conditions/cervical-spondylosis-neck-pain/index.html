From an Ayurvedic perspective

Known as Griva Sandhigata Vata or Manyasthambha, cervical spondylosis arises when sedentary posture, forward head tilt, and mental stress dry up the cervical discs and tighten the trapezius and levator scapulae muscles. Our protocols combine local oil reservoirs (Griva Basti) with Nasya (nasal administration) to clear neurological channels above the clavicle.

How we assess your condition

Cervical posture analysis, evaluation of radiating numbness/tingling in fingers, range-of-motion testing, and pulse diagnosis.

Ayurvedic approaches your physician may consider

Our cervical spine care strategy targets both nerve decompression and muscular relaxation: 1. Localised pooling of warm therapeutic oils over C4-C7 vertebrae (Griva Basti) to soothe irritated nerve roots and relieve trapezium stiffness. 2. Nasal instillation of potent neuro-protective oils (Nasya) to directly reach cervical and cranial nerve micro-channels. 3. Classical internal nervine Rasayanas to halt progressive disc dehydration and prevent cervicogenic headaches.

Physician-prescribed therapies may include:

  • Kizhi

  • Abhyangam

  • Shirodhara

  • Panchakarma

Important Note: Individual treatment plans depend on cervical MRI findings and clinical presentation.
Emergency Notice: Seek appropriate medical evaluation for severe, sudden, worsening, or concerning symptoms. Ayurvedic care should not be presented as a substitute for emergency medical care.
