From an Ayurvedic perspective

Classical Ayurveda classifies skin conditions under Kushta Roga, stemming from deep-rooted impurities in Rasa, Rakta (blood), and Mamsa (muscle) tissues caused by impaired liver metabolism and chronic stress. Instead of relying on topical corticosteroids that lead to rebound flare-ups, we cleanse the system internally through Shodhana (detoxification) and Takradhara (medicated buttermilk stream).

How we assess your condition

Skin lesion morphology examination, trigger-factor mapping, liver metabolic assessment, and Nadi Pariksha.

Ayurvedic approaches your physician may consider

Our dermatological management addresses internal blood and metabolic purity: 1. Deep hepatic and blood channel purification (Raktamokshana and Virechana) to purge accumulated Pitta-Kapha toxins. 2. External soothing and plaque softening via medicated buttermilk irrigation (Takradhara) and herbal washes. 3. Long-term internal blood-purifying Rasayanas coupled with strict exclusion of inflammatory food triggers.

Physician-prescribed therapies may include:

  • Panchakarma

  • Kerala Chikitsa

  • Abhyangam

Important Note: Skin treatments require disciplined adherence to dietary guidelines (avoiding incompatible foods/Viruddha Ahara).
Emergency Notice: Seek appropriate medical evaluation for severe, sudden, worsening, or concerning symptoms. Ayurvedic care should not be presented as a substitute for emergency medical care.
