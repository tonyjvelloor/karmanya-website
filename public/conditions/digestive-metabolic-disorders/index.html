From an Ayurvedic perspective

Ayurveda considers Agni (digestive fire) the foundational pillar of all health. When Agni is disturbed (Mandagni or Vishamagni), incompletely digested toxins called Ama enter systemic circulation, manifesting as Grahani (IBS), Amlapitta (acid reflux), and chronic fatigue. Treatment focuses on Dipana-Pachana (kindling digestive enzymes), gut cleansing, and gut microbiome revitalization.

How we assess your condition

Thorough dietary history, stool pattern analysis, tongue diagnosis (Jihva Pariksha), pulse assessment, and lifestyle rhythm review.

Ayurvedic approaches your physician may consider

Our digestive restoration protocol addresses the fundamental bio-fire (Agni): 1. Deep cellular digestive detox to digest and clear toxic metabolic waste (Ama) using customized carminative formulations (Deepana-Pachana). 2. Targeted systemic cleansing through physician-guided Virechana or Basti to reset gut motility and microbiome balance. 3. Strict constitutional dietary guidelines (Ahara Vidhi) to prevent recurrence of IBS, bloating, and metabolic stagnation.

  • Kerala Chikitsa
