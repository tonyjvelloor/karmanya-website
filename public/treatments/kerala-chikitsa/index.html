Traditional Ayurvedic Perspective

Kerala's Ashtavaidya lineage developed specialized modalities combining deep tissue immersion with high-potency herbal decoctions for severe neuromuscular and degenerative conditions.

What happens during treatment?

Includes master therapies: Pizhichil (continuous warm oil bath), Njavarakizhi (medicinal rice and milk bolus sudation), and Udhwarthanam (herbal powder lymphatic massage).

What is it commonly used for?

Neurological conditions, severe joint pain, muscular atrophy, hemiplegia, and metabolic resets.

Is this treatment right for you?
Specific therapies are chosen strictly based on Nadi Pariksha and physician consultation.
