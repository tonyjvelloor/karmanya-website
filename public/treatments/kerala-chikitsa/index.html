Traditional Ayurvedic Perspective

Kerala's unique Ashtavaidya tradition developed highly specialized therapies combining deep tissue treatments with potent herbal preparations. Unlike generic spa massages, these are rigorous clinical protocols globally renowned for their efficacy in managing chronic, neurological, and degenerative conditions. Our therapies use authentic oils imported from Kerala, warmed to specific temperatures, and applied with precise rhythmic strokes by traditionally trained therapists.

What happens during treatment?

Our Kerala Chikitsa portfolio includes several major therapies: • ABHYANGAM: A specialized full-body massage using warm, Dosha-specific medicated oils. It deeply nourishes the tissues (Dhatus), improves circulation, and pacifies aggravated Vata. • SHIRODHARA: The continuous, rhythmic pouring of warm medicated oil, herbal decoctions, or buttermilk over the forehead (Ajna Chakra). It induces a profound state of relaxation and is a primary clinical tool for stress, anxiety, insomnia, and neurological disorders. • PIZHICHIL (Sarvangadhara): Often called the 'Treatment of Kings', this involves squeezing warm medicated oil from cloth continuous over the entire body while simultaneously massaging it. It is unmatched for severe Vata disorders, muscular degeneration, and joint stiffness. • NJAVARAKIZHI: A unique sudation therapy where a special type of medicinal rice (Njavara) is cooked with herbal decoctions and milk, tied in boluses, and applied rhythmically over the body. It strengthens muscles, nourishes the nerves, and is highly effective for paralysis and muscle wasting. • UDHVARTHANAM: A vigorous deep-tissue massage using dry herbal powders. It is the primary Ayurvedic clinical intervention for lymphatic drainage, breaking down subcutaneous fat (Kapha reduction), and improving metabolic sluggishness.

What is it commonly used for?

These therapies form the core of our management protocols for neurological conditions, severe joint pain, stress disorders, muscular degeneration, and metabolic resets.

Is this treatment right for you?
Specific Kerala therapies (like Shirodhara or Pizhichil) are selected strictly based on your Nadi Pariksha (pulse diagnosis) and current Dosha assessment. Not all therapies are suitable for all patients. An initial consultation is mandatory.
