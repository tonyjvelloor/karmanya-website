Traditional Ayurvedic Perspective

Panchakarma translates to 'five actions'. It is the ultimate Shodhana (purification) therapy in Ayurveda, designed to eliminate deep-rooted Ama (toxins) and balance the Doshas.

What happens during treatment?

The process is highly personalized. It involves Purvakarma (preparatory procedures like internal oleation and Swedana), Pradhana Karma (the main detoxification therapies like Basti or Virechana), and Paschatkarma (post-treatment restorative regimens).

What is it commonly used for?

Utilized for chronic metabolic conditions, severe musculoskeletal issues, long-term skin disorders, and comprehensive systemic rejuvenation.

Is this treatment right for you?
Panchakarma is an intensive clinical procedure. It requires a thorough medical evaluation by our chief physician. It is absolutely contraindicated during pregnancy, extreme weakness, and acute illnesses.
