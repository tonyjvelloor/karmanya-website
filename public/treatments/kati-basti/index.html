Spinal & Lumbosacral Therapy

Kati Basti Spinal Care

Specialized therapeutic oil pooling over the lumbar and sacral spine to soothe inflamed nerves, rehydrate discs, and eliminate chronic back pain.

MEDICALLY REVIEWED
Last reviewed: September 2026 | Reviewed by: Dr. Irshad T.M.

Traditional Ayurvedic Perspective

Kati Basti specifically addresses aggravated Apana and Vyana Vata in the lumbosacral region. By pooling warm lipophilic herbal preparations over the lower back, tissue stiffness is resolved, intervertebral spacing is decompressed, and muscle guarding softens.

What happens during treatment?

A dough ring of herbal gram flour is placed securely over the affected lumbar vertebrae (L3-S1). Warm anti-inflammatory herbal oils are poured and maintained at a constant therapeutic temperature for 35 to 45 minutes.

What is it commonly used for?

Lumbar spondylosis, slip disc (disc bulge/herniation), sciatica (Gridhrasi), coccydynia, and postural lumbar strain.

Is this treatment right for you?
Not recommended during acute lumbar spinal trauma or fractures. Pre-assessment with recent MRI/X-ray is conducted.

Conditions this therapy may support

Emergency Notice: Seek appropriate medical evaluation for severe, sudden, worsening, or concerning symptoms. Ayurvedic care should not be presented as a substitute for emergency medical care.
