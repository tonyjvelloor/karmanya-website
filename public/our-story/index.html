✦ THE FOUNDING STORY & CLINICAL MISSION ✦

Why We Started Karmanya Ayurveda

Founded by Dr. Anandhu & Dr. Aditya — Bringing Authentic Kerala Ashtavaidya Chikitsa to Pune

Two friends. Two different journeys. One shared conviction in the profound healing science of authentic Kerala Ayurveda.

Every clinical institution begins with a choice.

Some begin as business ventures designed around fast commercial packages, retail shelves, and day-spa footfall.

And then there are clinical practices that begin with a shared calling.

Karmanya Ayurveda began with a calling.

A calling to prove that classical Ayurveda is not a cosmetic luxury, not an alternative to be tried when all else fails, and not a quick-fix massage counter.

Ayurveda is an intricate, time-tested medical science — developed over millennia, codified in classical Samhitas, and preserved with unparalleled clinical precision in the Kerala Ashtavaidya tradition.

We founded Karmanya Ayurveda to bring that uncompromised medical rigor to Pune.

Before Karmanya, There Was a Friendship

Our journey began years before Karmanya had a clinic or a name.

We first met as young students during our graduation in Ayurveda.

Dr. Anandhu was born in Kerala, immersed in its rich Ayurvedic heritage, but grew up right here in Pune. He understood both worlds — the traditional healing sanctuaries of God's Own Country and the fast-paced, high-pressure urban life of Pune.

Dr. Aditya brought his own perspective from Mumbai — another city shaped by relentless ambition, long commutes, and modern urban lifestyle stress.

As we studied the classical Ayurvedic texts together, we realized that ancient Ayurvedic thinking was not merely historical — it was desperately needed in the modern world.

Modern routines have changed. Desk work has exploded. Sleep cycles are disrupted. Stress has multiplied. Yet the human body's biological need for balance and authentic healing remains unchanged.

Why Pune?

For us, Pune was never just a pin code. For Dr. Anandhu, Pune was home.

Across Hinjawadi, Baner, Wakad, and PCMC, we saw hard-working IT professionals in their 20s and 30s living with severe cervical spasms, sciatica, and chronic fatigue. We saw active parents in their 50s suffering from debilitating knee pain, told that invasive surgery was their only option. We saw women trapped in cycles of hormonal imbalance and PCOS.

When these people sought Ayurvedic care, what they found was deeply disappointing:

Commercial day spas masquerading as "Ayurveda centres" with generic oils and untrained masseurs. Or commercial chains aggressively pushing rigid multi-week discount packages without diagnostic evaluation.

We wanted to build something different: A true medical sanctuary where consultation comes before commerce, and understanding comes before treatment.

Why We Chose a Single Centre at Pimple Saudagar

From the beginning, we chose not to create a franchise chain. In classical Kerala Ayurveda, the physician is the heart of the healing process.

When medicine is franchised into mass outlets, diagnostic rigor vanishes. That is why we invested everything into a single, fully equipped medical facility at 27/11 Swaraj Garden Road, Pimple Saudagar:

  • Direct Physician Assessment: Dr. Irshad and Dr. Tejasvi personally evaluate every patient, read diagnostic scans, and monitor therapeutic responses daily.
  • Monolithic Kerala Droni Chambers: Handcrafted treatment tables carved from single logs of medicinal Anjili or Neem timber imported from Kerala.
  • 100% Genuine GMP Pharmacy Oils: Formulations sourced directly from Kerala's premier institutions — Arya Vaidya Sala Kottakkal and Vaidyaratnam Oushadhasala.
  • Trained Kerala Therapists: Certified therapists applying authentic techniques with strict clinical oversight.

Our Core Clinical Charter

The Karmanya Principles:

  1. Diagnosis Before Therapy: Comprehensive 45-minute clinical assessment including Nadi Pariksha, scan review, and constitutional analysis.
  2. No Arbitrary Packages: Healing is biological, not transactional. Therapy frequency is determined by tissue response, never forced packages.
  3. Root-Cause Resolution: Treating underlying metabolic toxins (Ama) and Dosha vitiations rather than masking symptoms.
  4. Medical Transparency: If surgical or allopathic care is needed, our physicians will guide you openly and honestly.
