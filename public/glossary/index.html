CLINICAL MEDICAL REFERENCE

Ayurvedic Medical Glossary

Clear, medically accurate definitions of classical Ayurvedic terminology, diagnostic sciences, and treatment modalities practiced at Karmanya.

Ayurveda is not merely herbal remedies; it is a complete, codified medical system with its own anatomical, physiological, and pharmacological terminology. This glossary serves to demystify these terms for our patients. All treatments are conducted exclusively at our medical centre: 27/11, Swaraj Garden Road, Pimple Saudagar, Pune.

Abhyangam

Therapies
Abhyaṅgam

Synchronised full-body medicinal oil application performed by trained therapists following specific lymphatic and nerve pathways, followed by herbal steam (Swedana) to loosen deep tissue toxins.

→ View Abhyangam Details

Agni

Physiology
Agni

The biological and digestive fire responsible for enzymatic digestion, cellular metabolism, nutrient absorption, and immune resistance. Healthy Agni prevents disease formation.

→ Metabolic Protocols

Ama

Pathology
Āma

Toxic, sticky, undigested metabolic byproduct that forms in the gastrointestinal tract due to weakened digestive fire (Mandaagni), circulating through micro-channels (Srotas) and triggering systemic inflammation and auto-immune reactions.

→ Digestive Disorder Care

Ashtavaidya

Tradition
Aṣṭavaidya

The revered Kerala Ayurvedic physician lineage that mastered all eight classical branches (Ashtanga) of Ayurveda, renowned worldwide for developing specialized oil procedures and curative chikitsa traditions.

→ Dr. Irshad's Background

Basti

Panchakarma
Basti

Administration of herbal decoctions (Kashaya Basti) or medicated oils (Sneha Basti) into the rectum. Recognized in classical texts as the 'half of all medical treatment' because it directly controls Vata dosha at its prime anatomical seat.

→ Sciatica Spine Protocols

Griva Basti

Therapies
Grīvā Basti

Localised pooling of warm therapeutic herbal oil over the cervical vertebrae of the neck to nourish intervertebral discs, relieve muscle spasm, and resolve cervical spondylosis and cervicogenic headaches.

→ Cervical Spondylosis Care

Janu Basti

Therapies
Jānu Basti

A specialised non-invasive Panchakarma upakarma where a reservoir of black gram flour dough is placed around the knee joint and filled with warm medicated oils (such as Mahanarayana Taila) to regenerate synovial lubrication, soothe cartilage wear, and treat osteoarthritis.

→ View Janu Basti Protocol

Kapha Dosha

Doshas
Kapha Doṣa

The biological anabolic principle composed of Earth and Water elements. Governs physical structure, joint lubrication (Shleshaka Kapha), tissue cohesion, and weight stability. Excess causes fluid retention, cysts, and metabolic sluggishness.

→ Kapha & Hormonal Health

Kati Basti

Therapies
Kaṭī Basti

An intensive spinal therapy where a dough dam is constructed over the lumbosacral region (L4-L5/S1) and filled with warm medicated herbal oil for 45 minutes to relieve nerve root compression, disc herniation, and sciatica.

→ View Kati Basti Protocol

Nadi Pariksha

Diagnostics
Nāḍī Parīkṣā

Classical Ayurvedic pulse diagnosis technique performed at the radial artery using three fingers to assess the qualitative balance of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas, evaluate metabolic fire (Agni), tissue health (Dhatus), and detect subclinical disease before symptoms manifest.

→ Experienced by our physicians

Nasya

Panchakarma
Nasya

Administration of therapeutic herbal oils or powders through the nasal passages. Described in classical Ayurveda as the direct channel to the brain and head, effective for cervical spondylosis, migraine, sinusitis, and sleep disorders.

→ Panchakarma Modalities

Njavara Kizhi

Therapies
Ñavarakkizhi

A unique Kerala Ashtavaidya rejuvenation therapy where special organic medicinal rice (Shashtika Shali) cooked in herbal milk decoction is tied in cloth boluses and applied across the body to restore wasted muscle and nourish nerve roots.

→ Kerala Chikitsa Tradition

Ojas

Physiology
Ojas

The supreme subtle essence of all seven bodily tissues (Dhatus), representing vital resilience, innate immunity, mental stability, and longevity in classical Ayurvedic medicine.

→ Restoring Vital Resilience

Panchakarma

Therapies
Pañcakarma

The premier five-fold classical biological detoxification protocol in Ayurveda: Vamana (emesis), Virechana (purgation), Basti (medicated enema), Nasya (nasal therapy), and Raktamokshana (bloodletting), designed to purge deep cellular metabolic waste (Ama).

→ View Panchakarma Details

Patra Pinda Sweda (Kizhi)

Therapies
Patra Piṇḍa Sveda

A therapeutic fomentation procedure using boluses filled with medicinal herbal leaves fried in medicated oils, applied rhythmically over stiff joints and muscles to reduce swelling, spasm, and arthritic pain.

→ View Kizhi Therapy

Pitta Dosha

Doshas
Pitta Doṣa

The biological thermal principle composed of Fire and Water elements. Governs enzymatic digestion, cellular thermogenesis, hepatic metabolism, and hormone synthesis. Imbalance causes inflammatory skin diseases, acidity, and burning sensations.

→ Pitta Skin Conditions

Prakriti

Physiology
Prakṛti

An individual's unique genetic and constitutional bio-type determined at conception by the proportion of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas, dictating metabolic traits and disease predispositions.

→ Consult on Your Prakriti

Rasayana

Therapies
Rasāyana

Rejuvenation and longevity therapeutics in classical Ayurveda that enhance cellular nutrition, prevent premature tissue degeneration, promote tissue regeneration, and boost immunity.

→ View All Therapies

Shirodhara

Therapies
Śirodhārā

The continuous, rhythmic pouring of a stream of warm herbal oil or medicated buttermilk (Takradhara) across the forehead over the Ajna Marma. Proven to activate parasympathetic nervous system response, lower cortisol, and relieve insomnia and anxiety.

→ View Shirodhara Protocol

Srotas

Physiology
Srotas

The macroscopic and microscopic bodily channel networks through which biological fluids, nutrients, and waste products flow. Clearing blocked channels (Sroto-shodhana) is a core aim of Panchakarma.

→ Channel Cleansing Therapy

Uttara Basti

Panchakarma
Uttara Basti

A gentle specialised Panchakarma procedure performed by female physicians where medicated oils are administered into the intrauterine tract to treat PCOD, thin endometrium, tubal blockage, and recurrent reproductive disorders.

→ Female Healthcare Protocols

Vata Dosha

Doshas
Vāta Doṣa

The biological kinetic principle composed of Space and Air elements. Governs all neuro-muscular movement, breathing, nerve transmission, and joint articulation. When aggravated, causes dryness, joint pain, sciatica, and degeneration.

→ Vata Joint Conditions

Vikriti

Pathology
Vikṛti

The current state of dosha imbalance or pathological deviation away from an individual's natural constitutional baseline (Prakriti), evaluated during clinical consultation to plan therapy.

→ Conditions We Treat

Virechana

Panchakarma
Virecana

Medically controlled therapeutic purgation using classical herbal decoctions and castor preparations to cleanse Pitta dosha and toxins from the liver, gallbladder, and small intestine. Highly effective for psoriasis, eczema, and PCOD.

→ Psoriasis & Skin Care
