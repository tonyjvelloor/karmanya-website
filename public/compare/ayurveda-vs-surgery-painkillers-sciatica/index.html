Why Masking Nerve Pain With Painkillers Accelerates Disc Damage

Pain is your body's protective biofeedback mechanism. When heavy NSAIDs, muscle relaxants, or pregabalin mute pain signals, patients continue lifting, sitting in poor postures, and stressing the damaged disc. Over months, this turns a minor disc bulge into an extruded disc or permanent nerve root impingement.

At Karmanya Ayurveda, our goal is not temporary sedation of symptoms. By pooling warm classical herbal oils (Ksheerabala Taila, Murivenna) directly over the affected lumbar vertebrae in Kati Basti, we induce deep vascular perfusion, reduce perineural swelling, and relieve the severe muscular spasms immobilising the lower spine.

Red Flags: When Is Spinal Surgery Immediately Necessary?

Patient safety is paramount. If a patient experiences Cauda Equina Syndrome (sudden loss of bladder or bowel control, saddle anaesthesia, or sudden progressive foot drop/paralysis), this constitutes a medical emergency requiring immediate surgical decompression within 24–48 hours. Our physicians screen every back pain patient during physical assessment to rule out these emergency criteria.
